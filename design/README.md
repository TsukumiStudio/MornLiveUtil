# MornLiveUtil 画像素材

- `icon.png`: 1024 × 1024、不透明。Codex の組み込み画像生成で制作。
- `icon.png` から作った `Support/AppIcon.icns`（16〜512px と各 2 倍）を `make-app.sh` でアプリに同梱する。アイコンを変えたら ICNS も作り直す。

## 生成プロンプト

Use case: logo-brand. Create a finished macOS app icon for MornLiveUtil, a utility that reads live-stream chat comments aloud. Square 1024x1024, fully opaque solid uniform pale cream background filling the whole canvas, no transparency, no checkerboard, no external shadow. Centered with generous inset margin: one large rounded speech bubble in deep coral, containing three short horizontal rounded bars (chat text lines) in cream; to the upper right of the bubble, three concentric curved sound-wave arcs in warm orange, growing outward. Flat clean geometric graphic, large uniform solid fills, no gradients, no outlines. Recognizable at 32px. No text, no letters, no numbers, no play button, no YouTube logo or red play triangle, no characters, no microphone. No paper texture, grain, speckles, halftone or noise.
